ECONUR Store Audit

A full review of econur.shop before handover: what is working, what needs fixing, and what to do next. Every finding below was measured on the live store, not estimated.

Website econur.shop Audit date 4 October 2026 Platform WordPress 7.1.2 · WooCommerce 11.1.2 · Astra + ECONUR child theme Checked desktop and phone (360–1920 px)

Summary

The shop itself is in good shape. Design, mobile layout, product pages, cart, Buy Now and Cash on Delivery checkout all work and were tested end to end. The software is up to date and runs on HTTPS.

Four things must be fixed before spending money on ads: the legal pages are not published, there is no sales tracking (Google Analytics / Meta Pixel), the "Flat 60% OFF" banner is not backed by real sale prices, and some product claims are stronger than the products can support. Order emails are also at risk of landing in spam.

Findings

Critical

Legal pages are not published

Owner: store owner (content) + developer (links)
Found
Privacy Policy and Refund & Returns Policy exist only as drafts. No Terms & Conditions page is set in WooCommerce.
Why it matters
Customers cannot see return or privacy terms before paying. Meta and Google ad accounts, and Google / Facebook login, require a published privacy policy.
Fix
Review the two drafts against how the business really works (return window, who pays delivery, COD rules), publish them, create a Terms page, link all three in the footer and at checkout.
Critical

No sales tracking

Owner: developer
Found
No Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel or Meta Conversions API on any page checked (homepage, shop, product, cart, checkout).
Why it matters
There is no way to know where visitors come from, which products sell, or whether an ad paid for itself. Facebook / Instagram ads cannot optimise for purchases.
Fix
Install GA4 and Meta Pixel with server-side Conversions API (official WooCommerce integrations), with view, add-to-cart, checkout and purchase events. Test with a real order.
Critical

"Flat 60% OFF" is not backed by real prices

Owner: store owner (decision) + developer
Found
The homepage banner says "Flat 60% OFF" with a countdown to 8 October, 14:16. Products still sell at full price (for example ৳280). The only sale prices are Herbifresh 100 gm (৳1,000 → ৳99) and one Olivelle option (৳540 → ৳200).
Why it matters
Customers who click the offer and see full prices feel misled. Ad platforms reject ads that promise a discount the landing page does not show.
Fix
Either set real 60% sale prices on the chosen products until the countdown ends, or switch to the neutral banner (one setting, already built).
High

Product claims are stronger than they can support

Owner: store owner
Found
  • Licorice: "Fades sunburn and tan spots", "Clears acne marks".
  • Olivelle: "Safe for babies and young children".
  • Site title and footer: "100% natural, chemical-free", although the bars are made with lye (saponified).
  • Herbifresh is described as "fragrance-free" but contains tea tree and lavender essential oils.
  • Product pages show "5.0 · Based on 3 customer reviews" next to "Reviews (0)": those 3 are store testimonials, not reviews of that product.
Why it matters
Cosmetic claims that sound medical or absolute can break consumer-protection rules and get ads rejected. Contradictions lower trust.
Fix
Use the gentler wording already on Active Defense ("helps", "feels"). Correct the fragrance wording. Label testimonials as store reviews, or show them only once the product has its own reviews.
High

Order emails may land in spam

Owner: developer
Found
Store emails go out with an empty sender name, from pl.proguidance@gmail.com, sent through the web host. The domain has SPF and a DMARC record set to "none".
Why it matters
Gmail and Yahoo treat mail that claims to come from a Gmail address but is sent by another server as suspicious. Customers may miss order confirmations.
Fix
Send as "ECONUR" from an @econur.shop mailbox (the domain already has Hostinger mail), with authenticated SMTP and DKIM. Raise DMARC to "quarantine" once mail passes.
High

Price and option errors

Owner: store owner
Found
Olivelle has two options both called "100 gm" (৳300 and ৳540 → ৳200), so the shop shows "৳300 – ৳540". Herbifresh 100 gm shows "Save 90%" (৳1,000 → ৳99).
Fix
Delete the duplicate Olivelle option and confirm the Herbifresh price. These look like data-entry mistakes.
High

SEO and share previews are missing

Owner: developer + store owner (text)
Found
No SEO plugin. None of the 7 pages checked has a meta description or Open Graph tags. Product schema exists on product pages; the homepage has none. The page language is set to English although most text is Bangla.
Why it matters
Links shared on Facebook, WhatsApp and Messenger show no image or description. Google writes its own snippets.
Fix
Install Rank Math or Yoast, write a title and description for the homepage, categories and each product, set a share image, add Organization schema.
High

Security hardening and handover access

Owner: developer + store owner
Found
  • XML-RPC is open.
  • The admin username can be read from the public REST API.
  • The theme/plugin file editor is on, and plugin auto-updates are off.
  • 5 plugin updates are pending: Elementor, Hostinger AI, Hostinger Easy Onboarding, Hostinger Reach and Novamira.
  • The only admin account is the developer's.
  • The Novamira plugin lets an AI assistant run code on the site; it was used to build this store.
Fix
  • Create the client's own admin account, and remove or downgrade the developer account after handover.
  • Deactivate Novamira unless ongoing remote maintenance is agreed.
  • Disable XML-RPC, hide user listing and turn off the file editor.
  • Apply the pending updates and enable auto-updates for minor versions.
Medium

Page speed

Owner: developer
Found
  • The server takes 0.65–0.9 s to start sending an uncached page (cached visits are faster).
  • The homepage makes about 79 requests on a phone, including 26 style and 24 script files.
  • Style files total about 630–910 KB before compression, and fonts about 350–400 KB.
  • On desktop the homepage loads about 2.3 MB of photos, because product cards use 1024 px images.
  • LiteSpeed CSS/JS minify, WebP conversion and lazy-load are switched off.
Fix
  • Merge the four design style layers into one file.
  • Trim unused font weights.
  • Serve 600 px card images.
  • Turn on LiteSpeed minify and WebP one at a time, testing checkout after each.
Medium

Trust content

Owner: store owner
Found
0 product reviews. No About page. "Contact" in the menu opens WhatsApp only, and the store address in WooCommerce is empty. Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp links are present.
Fix
Ask every delivered customer for a review (WhatsApp message with the product link; the review form is ready). Add a short About page (who makes the bars, where) and a Contact page with phone, WhatsApp and business address.
Medium

Product data gaps

Owner: store owner
Found
  • No SKUs or weights on any product.
  • Natural Scalp Shampoo and Natural Toothpaste are "coming soon", with no price, ingredients or usage.
  • Olivelle has only one photo.
  • Every bar is shown in the same beige pouch on the same background, so the product cards look alike.
  • Only Active Defense has recorded sales (4 units).
Fix
Add SKUs and weights (needed for courier and stock control). Complete the two coming-soon products before launch. Shoot extra photos per bar, ideally with a different colour accent for each (charcoal, turmeric, coffee, green tea, olive).
Medium

Open orders

Owner: store owner
Found
3 orders (৳1,300 total, placed 23 September – 3 October) are still in "Processing".
Fix
Mark delivered orders as "Completed" so customers get the completion email and sales reports are correct.
Low

Housekeeping

Owner: developer
Found
  • Unused plugins: Jetpack (inactive), WPForms Lite (no forms), WPCode (draft snippets only).
  • WordPress memory limit is 40 MB; WooCommerce recommends 256 MB.
  • Images missing alt text: 9 on the homepage and 16 on a product page (mostly decorative).
  • Coupons are switched on but none exist.
  • Hostinger Reach (email marketing) is installed but not set up.
  • Google / Facebook login is installed but switched off until the privacy policy is published.
Fix
Remove the unused plugins, raise the memory limit, add alt text to product images, and set up a welcome / abandoned-cart email once tracking is live.
